FAQ
Common questions
Is Nkɔsoɔ AI really free? ▼
Yes. Weather, market prices, pest alerts, and the Ask AI (3 questions/day) are completely free with no account needed. Creating a free account unlocks your crop and livestock profiles, planting calendars, farm diary, credit score, GPS location, and the marketplace. Pro (GHS 30/month) adds unlimited AI questions, photo diagnosis, and voice interface.
Do I need a smartphone? ▼
Any phone with a web browser works — Android, iPhone, or basic smartphone. No app download needed. The platform is designed to work on low-bandwidth connections common in rural Ghana.
How does the marketplace payment work? ▼
All payments go through Paystack — Ghana's trusted payment gateway. Buyers pay with MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, Visa, or Mastercard. For produce sales, payment is held in escrow for 48 hours after the buyer confirms delivery, then released to the farmer. Instant release is available at a 2% fee. Nkɔsoɔ AI takes a 5% commission on produce sales and 3% on farm inputs.
Who can list on the marketplace? ▼
Any farmer registered on the platform with a valid Ghana Card can list their harvest or livestock for sale. They must complete 4 training modules and agree to the seller terms before going live. Input suppliers register separately at nkosooai.com/supplier/register and are reviewed by DranyTech before being approved.
Is my Ghana Card information safe? ▼
Your Ghana Card number is stored securely and used only for identity verification — to confirm you are a real Ghanaian farmer. It is never shared with third parties without your consent. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
