What is live on the platform today

Built with Python, Flask, SQLite, Paystack, and OpenStreetMap. Deployed on Render at www.nkosooai.com.

🌤
Live weather · 16 Ghana regions Free
7-day forecasts with specific crop and livestock advice per region. Updates every 30 minutes via Open-Meteo.
📈
Market prices · crops and livestock Free
GHS prices for 8 crops and 8 livestock types. AI-refreshed weekly based on current season and market conditions in Kumasi and Accra.
🌾
My Crops profile Free account
Ghana Card verification · farm size and unit · soil type · water source · 14 staple crops + 9 fruit options · AI planting calendar per crop · farm diary with AI tips.
🐄
Livestock profiles · 6 animal types · multiple per farmer Free account
Poultry · Cattle · Goats · Sheep · Pigs · Aquaculture. Ghana Card linked to livestock ownership. AI vaccination and health calendar. Livestock diary with AI tips per entry.
🔬
Verified agronomist directory Free account
Credential-verified experts · filter by speciality · WhatsApp booking · admin verification system · 10% platform commission on consultations.
🐛
Pest and disease alerts Free
Crop and livestock pest and disease alerts with local treatment tips in English and Twi.
📷
AI photo diagnosis Pro
Upload crop or animal photo · AI identifies disease in seconds · treatment advice with locally available products.
🎤
Voice interface · 4 languages Pro
English microphone input. Twi, Dagbani, Hausa — type your question and hear the answer read aloud. No literacy required.
🤖
AI farming chat 1/day free
Expert AI advice on crops, livestock, soil, pests, markets, and weather. 1 question/day free · 3/day with free account · unlimited with Pro.
💳
Credit access — farming activity score Free account
Farming activity score out of 100 · downloadable verified credit profile PDF · 4 verified agricultural lenders (Opportunity International, Sinapi Aba, Advans, ARB Apex) · loan application referral system.
📍
GPS farm & livestock location Free account
Exact farm coordinates captured via browser GPS · reverse geocoded address from OpenStreetMap · ±8m accuracy on mobile · GPS adds +10 to farming activity score · appears in credit profile PDF for lender risk assessment.
🐄
Multiple livestock types per farmer Free account
One farmer account supports Poultry, Cattle, Goats, Sheep, Pigs, and Fish/Aquaculture — each with its own profile, vaccination calendar, health diary, and GPS location. Add or remove animal types independently.
🏠
Farmer dashboard — My Farm Free account
Personal farm dashboard showing crops growing, animal types, diary entries, and credit score in one place. Activity feed with upcoming vaccinations, harvest windows, and recent AI activity. Sub-tabs for farm profile, livestock, and credit.
🏦
Agricultural lender directory — self-registration Open
Verified microfinance institutions register at nkosooai.com/register-lender · DranyTech reviews and verifies · verified lenders appear with loan range, interest rate, requirements, and WhatsApp button · Opportunity International, Sinapi Aba, Advans, ARB Apex listed.
